Basketball around the World!

A page about how Basketball impacts everyone around the World

Basketball is the third most popular sport in the world • When Dr. James Naismith invented the sport in 1891, the "hoops" were actually closed peach baskets. Every single time a player scored, a referee had to climb a ladder to manually retrieve the ball before play could resume.

Basketball is about 4.5 billion years old. It spins once a day, circles the Sun once a year, and carries about 8 billion people along for the ride.

Three facts about Earth

  • One trip around the Sun takes 365.25 days. That extra quarter day is why we have leap years.
  • The deepest known point in the ocean is about 11 kilometers down.
  • The Moon drifts away from Earth about 4 centimeters every year.
