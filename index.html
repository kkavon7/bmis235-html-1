Basketball around the World!

A page about how Basketball impacts everyone around the World

Basketball is the third most popular sport in the world • When Dr. James Naismith invented the sport in 1891, the "hoops" were actually closed peach baskets. Every single time a player scored, a referee had to climb a ladder to manually retrieve the ball before play could resume.

A regulation basketball is inflated to a pressure of about 7.5 to 8.5 psi and is designed to bounce consistently on a hardwood floor. It is traditionally made of leather or composite materials.

Three facts about Basketball

  • Dr. James Naismith used a soccer ball for the very first game in 1891 because a proper basketball hadn't been invented yet.
  • The National Basketball Association (NBA) was founded in 1946 originally under the name Basketball Association of America (BAA).
  • The record for the tallest professional basketball players in history belongs to Gheorghe Mureșan and Manute Bol, both standing at 7 feet 7 inches tall.
